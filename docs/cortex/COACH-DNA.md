# CORTEX — Coach DNA (rotation tendencies)

**Coach identities are not in our data.** No source for coaching staff has been ingested, so nothing is attributed to a
named coach. What exists is each team's observed rotation behaviour this season, shown in the team profile and in the
Knowledge Center as "Rotation tendencies (coach not identified)".

| Observation | Derivation |
|---|---|
| Rotation length | players with at least 10 minutes, per game: average and range |
| Starter minutes | average minutes of the five starters, games with confirmed starters |
| Bench points per game | points by non-starters, same games |
| Starting-five stability | number of distinct starting fives, and the three most used with game counts |
| Minutes leaders | minutes per game for the nine most-used players |
| First substitution | average time of the team's first substitution, from games observed live (count shown) |

Not derived yet: closing lineups, response to foul trouble, back-to-back and rest changes, score-differential
behaviour, overtime lineups. These need a season of live play-by-play; collection began on 2026-11-25.

These are observations of what happened. They are not statements about what a coach will do, and they must be read
with the roster, injuries and game context of the games they come from.
