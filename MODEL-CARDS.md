## What these results do and do not show

* They show how the models forecast outcomes relative to simple baselines on later, unseen dates.
* They do **not** show an advantage over sportsbook prices. That requires historical or forward market data; forward paper testing is the only evidence being collected.
* Several model variants were evaluated on the same data (baselines, challenger); the evaluation period is therefore not a pristine holdout for future model selection. Future promotions should be judged on data after the dates above.
